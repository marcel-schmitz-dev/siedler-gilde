In short: This website uses no tracking, no advertising and no analytics tools. Personal data is only processed when you visit (technical server data), when you register for the member area and when you email us. This English version is provided for convenience; if there is any discrepancy, the German version prevails.

Marcel Schmitz


Deutschland

Email: m.schmitz.dev@gmail.com

This is a private, non-commercial fan project of the “Die Siedler Online” guild “Gründer von Goldenau” [GvG]. No data protection officer has been appointed.

2. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA, a Microsoft company). When you open the site, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data, in particular your IP address, to deliver the site and keep the service secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest). Data may be transferred to the USA; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Registration, login and member area (Supabase)

Registration and login are provided by Supabase (Supabase, Inc., USA). You can use the public area without registering; the member area requires an account.

3.1 Data we store

  • Player name you enter when registering.
  • Email address used to sign in.
  • Password, stored only as a secure hash; I cannot see it.
  • User ID (randomly generated) and timestamps of registration and last sign-in.
  • Technical data such as IP address and browser data, as far as Supabase processes it to operate the service and prevent abuse (for example rate limiting of sign-in attempts).

3.2 Who can see what

Your player name is visible to other signed-in members. Your email address and password are not visible to other members. Visitors who are not signed in cannot see any member data.

3.3 Purpose, legal basis, retention

We process this data to give you access to the member area (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for security and abuse prevention, on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Providing the data is voluntary, but without it you cannot register. Data is kept until your account is deleted. To delete your account, email m.schmitz.dev@gmail.com; your profile is removed with it.

3.4 Processor and international transfers

Supabase processes the data on my behalf. The project is hosted in the region eu-west-1 . Because Supabase is a US company, access from a third country cannot be ruled out. Supabase offers a data processing agreement with EU Standard Contractual Clauses: More information: supabase.com/privacy.

4. Browser storage, no tracking cookies

After you sign in, the site stores a session token in your browser’s local storage so you stay signed in. It is deleted when you sign out. This is strictly necessary to provide the service you request (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG), so no consent is required. The website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and the JavaScript library for login is served by this site itself, not by a content delivery network.

5. Embedded content and external links

  • Combat simulator NEONFRONT is embedded from marcel-schmitz-dev.github.io (also GitHub Pages). Inputs are, to my knowledge, stored only locally in your browser.
  • Images from TSOwiki: when you open the detail view of a general, icons are loaded directly from tsowiki.eu, which transmits your IP address to that site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Mini games are separate sites and only linked. Their own privacy information applies. For the guild leaderboard in “Der fliegende Entdecker” you enter a name: Leaderboards are stored on Supabase

6. Contact by email

If you email me, I process your details and message to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once the matter is closed, unless legal retention duties apply.

Email me at m.schmitz.dev@gmail.com. There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

8. Security and updates

The site is delivered over HTTPS only. Passwords are stored only as hashes, and profile data is protected by Row Level Security: each member can only change their own profile. If features change, this policy will be updated. Last updated: November 2026.
