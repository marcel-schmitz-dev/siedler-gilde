2. Hosting (IONOS)

This website is hosted by IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Straße 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany. When you access the site, the hosting provider processes technically necessary connection data, in particular your IP address, date and time of the request, the requested file, status code, amount of data transferred and browser and operating system information (server log files), so that the site can be delivered and operated securely.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure, stable and efficient provision of the website). A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with IONOS. IONOS keeps log files by default for up to 8 weeks and deletes them afterwards. I do not analyse them. See the IONOS privacy policy (German).

3. Registration, sign-in and members area (Supabase)

For registration, sign-in and storage of profile data I use Supabase (provider: Supabase, Inc., USA, and affiliated companies). Without registering you can use the public area completely. The members area is only available after registration. Registration is voluntary, but without the data listed below I cannot set up an account for you.

Minimum age: Registration is reserved for persons aged 16 or over. You confirm your age with a checkbox when registering. I do not ask for a date of birth or proof of age, in order to collect as little data as possible. If I learn that an account was created by a person under 16, I delete it.

3.1 Data stored

  • Player name you enter at registration. Please choose a name you are comfortable with; your real name is not required.
  • Email address (for signing in).
  • Password: not stored in plain text, only as a secure hash. I cannot see your password.
  • User ID (randomly generated identifier) and the times of registration and last sign-in.
  • Age confirmation: the time at which you confirmed that you are at least 16 years old. It serves as proof that the confirmation was obtained.
  • Technical data when signing in and making requests, for example IP address and browser data, as far as Supabase processes it for operation and abuse protection (for example limiting too many sign-in attempts).

3.2 Who can see which data?

Your email address and password are not visible to other members. Visitors who are not signed in cannot see any member data. There is no public member list. If a list is introduced in the future, it will only be visible to signed-in members and I will update this policy beforehand.

3.3 Purpose and legal basis

The data is processed to give you access to the members area and to assign you a player name (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Processing of technical data for security and abuse prevention is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

3.4 Storage period and deletion

Your data remains stored until you have your account deleted. Send an email to kontakt@mschmitz-dev.de. When the account is deleted, your profile (player name) is removed as well.

3.5 Processor and third-country transfers

Supabase processes the data on my behalf under a data processing addendum (Art. 28 GDPR). The project is set up in the region eu-west-1 (Ireland), so the database is located in the European Union. As part of operation and support, Supabase may also process or access data in the USA and Singapore. The EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914), which are part of the data processing addendum, apply (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). More information: supabase.com/privacy and supabase.com/legal/dpa.

The website currently sends no automatic emails. If emails (for example confirmation or password reset) are introduced, this policy will be updated beforehand.

4. Browser storage, no tracking cookies

After you sign in, the website stores a session token in your browser’s local storage so that you stay signed in. It is deleted when you sign out. This access is technically necessary to provide the service you requested (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG), so consent is not required. Visitors who are not signed in have no data stored in their browser.

The website sets no cookies for advertising or analytics and integrates no analytics or tracking services. The JavaScript library for sign-in is delivered by this website itself; no fonts or scripts are loaded from content delivery networks.

5. External content in the members area and linked services

The following content is loaded only after sign-in in the members area. Visitors who are not signed in are not affected.

5.1 GitHub Pages: preview images and combat simulator

Some preview images (for example for the combat simulator and the mini game ByteRunner) are loaded from marcel-schmitz-dev.github.io. When you open the “Kampfsimulator” sub-page, the simulator is loaded as an embedded page from the same address. These pages are provided via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA, a Microsoft company). Your IP address is transmitted to GitHub (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, interest in displaying the content). See the GitHub Privacy Statement. To my knowledge, inputs and skill setups in the simulator are only stored locally in your browser and are not transmitted to me.

5.2 Images from TSOwiki

When you open the detail view of a general or explorer, icons and garrison images are loaded directly from tsowiki.eu. Your IP address is transmitted to the operator of that site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Without opening the detail view, this transfer does not take place.

5.3 Mini games and external links

The mini games are independent pages and are only linked. From the click on a link, the operators of the target site (for example GitHub or TSOwiki) process your data under their own privacy notices. The mini games and the combat simulator are currently being reworked. Should data be stored there in the future (for example a leaderboard), I will update this policy beforehand.

6. Contact

If you write to me by email or call me, I process your details (sender, contact data, content of the message) to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). I delete them once the request is dealt with and no statutory retention obligations apply.

7. Recipients

Recipients of your data are only the service providers named in this policy: IONOS (hosting), Supabase (sign-in and profile data) and, in the members area only, GitHub and TSOwiki (external content). I do not pass personal data on to anyone else, do not sell it and do not use it for advertising. There is no automated decision-making, including profiling.

8. Your rights

  • Access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Objection to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21 GDPR)

Right to object: Where I process data based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. I will then no longer process the data unless I can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds.

Simply send an email to kontakt@mschmitz-dev.de. I answer requests within one month.

10. Security

The website is delivered over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS). Passwords are stored only as a hash, and profile data is protected by access rules (Row Level Security) in the database: each member can only change their own profile.

11. Updates to this policy

If functions of the website change, I update this policy. The current version is always available here. Last updated: 16 November 2026.
